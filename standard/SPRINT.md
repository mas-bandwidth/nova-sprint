# NS-1: the nova-sprint standard

This is the normative standard of nova-sprint, the sprint machine: what the machine, its rows
and its coordinator do, stated as numbered requirements. Each section is its own file, in the
order below. The reasons behind each section, and the owner's words that set them, are the
rationale, NS-R, one file per section under `rationale/`. Section s1 states the key words and
the conventions every other section uses.
