# s5. Reads

A primary in review is read before it lands: each read is a read card, dealt to a reader that
did not work the attempt, which reads the attempt's head and closes the card ok or broken. This
section states how many reads an attempt needs, how and to whom the tick deals them, how a read
waits, and when a read card is taken back, replaced or judged. A read card's verdict closes the
read; how a primary is accepted on its reads and landed is [s6](s6.md).

## s5.1. The count and the parallel deal

<a id="R-READS-PARALLEL"></a>
**NS-5.1 R-READS-PARALLEL.** (hard) The tick MUST ask every read an attempt still needs in one
step, and MUST deal those reads at once, each to a different reader, as far as readers have room;
the deal of one read of an attempt MUST NOT wait for another read of the same attempt to be
taken, worked or closed.

<a id="R-READ-COUNT"></a>
**NS-5.2 R-READ-COUNT.** The number of reads the tick deals an attempt MUST be the sprint's read
count while one is set (0, 1, 2 or 3), and otherwise one for a card on the flash tier and two for
a card on any heavier tier.

<a id="R-READS-WANTED"></a>
**NS-5.3 R-READS-WANTED.** The tick MUST deal an attempt only the reads it still needs: its read
count less the reads that stand at the attempt (a read card placed, or closed with a verdict at
the attempt's head). The tick MUST NOT deal a read for an attempt while a read of it stands
broken, nor for an attempt whose work failed.

## s5.2. Who reads

<a id="R-READER-NEVER-WORKER"></a>
**NS-5.4 R-READER-NEVER-WORKER.** The tick MUST NOT deal a read card to the worker of the attempt
it reads.

<a id="R-READER-DISTINCT"></a>
**NS-5.5 R-READER-DISTINCT.** The tick MUST NOT deal a read of an attempt to a reader that holds
a read card of that attempt or closed one. A read the reader handed back, or let pass the read
deadline, counts as closed by it: the next read of the attempt goes to another reader.

<a id="R-READ-TIER"></a>
**NS-5.6 R-READ-TIER.** A read's tier is its primary's tier, raised to the read tier its stream or
the sprint sets when that one is stronger. The tick MUST NOT deal a read to a reader that does not
serve the read's tier or a stronger one: a friend is dealt a read only when one of its tiers is the
read's tier or stronger, and a member only when its reader row serves the read's tier.

<a id="R-READ-CHEAPEST"></a>
**NS-5.7 R-READ-CHEAPEST.** Among the readers that may take a read and have room, the tick MUST
deal it to one with a free lane before one with none, then to the reader whose tier is nearest at
or above the read's, then to the next such reader round a ring kept per tier in the store, so a
burst of reads rolls round every free reader and never lands twice on one while another is free.

<a id="R-READ-HALF-LANE"></a>
**NS-5.8 R-READ-HALF-LANE.** A read card on a member's row MUST hold half a lane of the member's
width: the member's load is its work cards and half its read cards, rounded up. A read card on a
friend's row MUST hold a whole lane of her width, as a work card does.

## s5.3. Waiting

<a id="R-READ-QUEUE-WAIT"></a>
**NS-5.9 R-READ-QUEUE-WAIT.** A read MUST wait in a row's ready queue only while every reader that
may take it is busy. A read with no reader that has room MUST be asked again on every tick until
it is dealt; waiting for room MUST NOT end the read or back off, and MUST NOT raise a judgment,
except that a frontier read waiting for room raises the no-frontier-room judgment.

<a id="R-READ-CANNOT-ASK"></a>
**NS-5.10 R-READ-CANNOT-ASK.** When no reader up may ever read an attempt (each is its worker, or
below its tier, or spent), the tick MUST raise one cannot-ask judgment for the attempt and MUST
NOT raise a second while that one is open. An acknowledged cannot-ask judgment whose review time
runs out while no reader may read the attempt is closed and raised again. The tick MUST close the
judgment on the tick a reader may read the attempt.

## s5.4. Take-backs, replacements and the branch

<a id="R-READ-TAKEBACK"></a>
**NS-5.11 R-READ-TAKEBACK.** A read card the machine takes back (its reader down, held or away, a
resting route, its primary moved off the attempt, or left untaken past the deal bound) MUST spend
nothing: its reader MAY be dealt the read again at a fresh generation, and MUST NOT be refused it
for good however often it is taken back.

<a id="R-READ-DEADLINE"></a>
**NS-5.12 R-READ-DEADLINE.** A read card working and not closed within the read deadline of its
start (its take; for a friend's read dealt straight to working, its deal) MUST be retired late,
spending its reader, and its read dealt to another reader. A read card left in ready MUST NOT be
retired late: past the deal bound it is taken back, spending no one.

<a id="R-READ-BRANCH-MISSING"></a>
**NS-5.13 R-READ-BRANCH-MISSING.** A broken verdict on a read whose branch origin does not hold at
its close MUST NOT count as a read: the read is retired spending no reader, and asked again on the
branch of the work that holds the head. When no branch of the work holds the head, the machine
MUST raise one judgment naming the read and the branch, and the tick MUST NOT deal the attempt a
read while that judgment is open; every decision the judgment offers MUST resolve it.

<a id="R-READ-REVIEW"></a>
**NS-5.14 R-READ-REVIEW.** A primary MUST NOT be accepted out of review while a read at its head
stands broken, nor before its reads stand: its read count of ok reads at its head from different
readers (the coordinator's own read at that head counting as one), or, for a script card, one ok
script read whose program verified the head. A primary in review with no broken read at its head
whose reads stand MUST be accepted.

## s5.5. State table: a read card

| State | Event | Guard | Action | Next state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (none) | tick | the attempt wants reads and a reader may take one with room | cut and deal the read card on the reader's row | ready (a member), working (a friend with a free lane) |
| ready | take | the reader's row is up and within its width | start the read | working |
| ready | tick | the deal bound passed since its deal, the primary left the attempt, its route rests, or its reader is down, held or away | take it back, spending no one | taken back |
| ready | tick | the rows are levelled and another reader that may take it has a free lane | take it back, spending no one, and deal it to that reader as a new card | taken back |
| working | verdict ok | the read names the attempt under review | close it ok | ok |
| working | verdict broken | the branch the read names is on origin | close it broken; raise the broken-read judgment | broken |
| working | verdict broken | the branch the read names is not on origin, and a branch of the work holds the head | retire it, spending no one; ask the read again on that branch | taken back |
| working | verdict broken | the branch the read names is not on origin, and no branch of the work holds the head | retire it, spending no one; raise the missing-branch judgment; deal the attempt no read while it is open | taken back |
| working | hand back | none | retire it, spending its reader | returned |
| working | tick | the read deadline passed since its start | retire it, spending its reader | late |
| working | tick | the primary left the attempt, its reader is down, held or away, or its member runs no child for it past the live grace | take it back, spending no one | taken back |

An event a state does not list is ignored in that state. Ok, broken, returned, late and taken
back are final for the card; a taken-back read is dealt again as a new card.

## s5.6. Bounds

| Bound | Value | Holds | On reaching it |
|---|---|---|---|
| read deadline | 60 minutes | a read card working, from its start | retired late, spending its reader; the read is dealt to another |
| deal bound | 6 hours by default (three times the take deadline), a setting of the sprint | a read card left in ready, from its deal | taken back, spending no one |
| live grace | 60 seconds | a read card working on a member whose beats do not name it | taken back, spending no one |

<a id="R-READ-BOUNDS"></a>
**NS-5.15 R-READ-BOUNDS.** The machine MUST hold each bound of the table above at the value it
gives.
