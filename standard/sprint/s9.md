# s9. The bus, hooks and pushes

Status: pending

Scope: the bus the machine pushes on, the hook every row holds and its challenge, and every event class the machine pushes and to whom.

## Hard rows

These rows are the owner's and bind while the rest of the section is pending.

<a id="R-HOOKED"></a>
**NS-9.1 R-HOOKED.** (hard) The coordinator and every friend up MUST hold a live hook for the
whole sprint, proven by the hook's challenge. The hook MUST challenge the coordinator every
10 minutes, independently of other traffic, and MUST challenge a silent friend within
10 minutes. The machine MUST NOT remove this challenge gate. A friend whose hook is missing, unproven or down
MUST be down and dealt nothing, and the coordinator MUST be told. Every verb the coordinator runs
MUST be refused, writing nothing, until its hook is proven, except the verbs of the machine's own
life, which cannot wait on a push the machine holds: `run` (the server), `tick` (its pass, but not
`tick --answer-rules`, which answers judgments as the coordinator), `server switch`, `seat install`
and `selftest land`; and `help` and `version`, which write nothing. No other verb is exempt.

<a id="R-PUSH-ONLY"></a>
**NS-9.2 R-PUSH-ONLY.** (hard) Every communication from the machine to a row MUST be a push to that row's hook. A friend, a member or the coordinator MUST NOT need to poll the store, a file or a verb, or to remember to look, to learn of an event it acts on: the machine MUST push each event class below to the rows it concerns.

<a id="R-PUSH-DEAL"></a>
**NS-9.3 R-PUSH-DEAL.** (hard) A card dealt to a row MUST be pushed to that row when it is dealt.

<a id="R-PUSH-READ"></a>
**NS-9.4 R-PUSH-READ.** (hard) A read card dealt to a row, or moved to it when the machine levels the rows, MUST be pushed to that row.

<a id="R-PUSH-JUDGMENT"></a>
**NS-9.5 R-PUSH-JUDGMENT.** (hard) Every judgment MUST be pushed to the coordinator once when it is first raised, and MUST be warned again on the bus while it waits unanswered.

<a id="R-PUSH-STUCK"></a>
**NS-9.6 R-PUSH-STUCK.** (hard) An operation the machine finds stuck MUST be pushed to the coordinator as its own class, naming the operation and where it is stuck.

<a id="R-PUSH-BOUND"></a>
**NS-9.7 R-PUSH-BOUND.** (hard) A bound reached MUST be pushed to the row that holds the card it bounds and to the coordinator, naming the bound.

<a id="R-PUSH-STALLED"></a>
**NS-9.8 R-PUSH-STALLED.** (hard) A stall (a card, a stream or the landing making no progress past its bound) MUST be pushed to the coordinator, naming what stalled.

<a id="R-PUSH-FINISH-DUE"></a>
**NS-9.9 R-PUSH-FINISH-DUE.** (hard) A card whose finish comes due MUST be pushed to the row working it before its deadline passes.

<a id="R-PUSH-FRIEND-STATE"></a>
**NS-9.10 R-PUSH-FRIEND-STATE.** (hard) A friend's change of state (up, down or held) MUST be pushed to the coordinator and to that friend.

<a id="R-PUSH-LANDING"></a>
**NS-9.11 R-PUSH-LANDING.** (hard) Every landing MUST be pushed to the coordinator when the base holds it.

<a id="R-PUSH-CI"></a>
**NS-9.12 R-PUSH-CI.** (hard) A result of continuous integration on a base or on a card's branch MUST be pushed to the coordinator and to the row that owns the card.
