# s2. Cards and briefs

Status: pending

Scope: what a card and its brief carry, how a brief is linted and admitted, and how needs and orders between cards are written.

## Hard rows

These rows are the owner's and bind while the rest of the section is pending.

<a id="R-MINIMAL-DEPS"></a>
**NS-2.1 R-MINIMAL-DEPS.** (hard) The writer of a card and the add and brief verbs MUST keep a card's needs the lightest that are still correct: a card MUST name a need only where it cannot compile or run without the need's code, and MUST write every other ordering as an order (land after). A stream MUST NOT be entirely serial by its needs unless there is no other way: the add and brief verbs MUST refuse a chain of three or more needs in which a link states no reason, naming the chain, and MUST show for each stream its longest chain of needs against its count of cards.

<a id="R-LINT-ON-ADD"></a>
**NS-2.2 R-LINT-ON-ADD.** (hard) Every verb and step that admits a brief or replaces one (an add by flag, by file, by directory or replacing a card; a brief edit; a twin; a recut; and every card the machine makes) MUST run the full card lint on the brief and MUST refuse a brief that fails it, writing nothing. A verb MUST NOT offer a flag that skips the lint.
