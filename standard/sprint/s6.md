# s6. Landing

Status: pending

Scope: how the land loop merges an accepted primary's head onto its base, gates it, pushes it, refuses it, and how streams and bases land side by side.

## Hard rows

These rows are the owner's and bind while the rest of the section is pending.

<a id="R-LAND-PARALLEL"></a>
**NS-6.1 R-LAND-PARALLEL.** (hard) The land loop MUST land each stream independently: one stream's landing MUST NOT wait on another stream's landing, gate or push, and cards of different streams, or of one stream on different bases, MUST merge and gate in parallel. Only cards that push to the same base MAY land one after another, and only for the push.
