# s3. The deal

Status: pending

Scope: how the tick moves cards from waiting to ready, deals ready cards to rows within their widths and tiers, splits cards that are too heavy, and how rows take what they are dealt.

## Hard rows

These rows are the owner's and bind while the rest of the section is pending.

<a id="R-PROMOTE"></a>
**NS-3.1 R-PROMOTE.** (hard) The tick MUST move a waiting card to ready on its next pass once every need the card names has landed, with no step of the coordinator.

<a id="R-SPLIT-HEAVY"></a>
**NS-3.2 R-SPLIT-HEAVY.** (hard) The machine MUST split a heavy card into pro cards wherever its work divides into parts that are each correct alone, as a step of its own, with no step of the coordinator.

<a id="R-SPLIT-PRO"></a>
**NS-3.3 R-SPLIT-PRO.** (hard) The machine MUST split a pro card into flash cards wherever its work divides into parts that are each correct alone, as a step of its own, with no step of the coordinator.

<a id="R-AUTO-TAKE"></a>
**NS-3.4 R-AUTO-TAKE.** (hard) A ready card dealt to a member's row or a friend's row MUST be taken and worked with no step of the coordinator: a member MUST take the ready cards of its row into every free lane itself, and a friend row's ready cards MUST be taken into its free lanes by the machine or by the friend's hook, and MUST NOT wait for a turn of the coordinator.
