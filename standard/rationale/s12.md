## R-CHECK-BEFORE-START (H9)

The owner, 2026-10-18: "Sprint check is always run before starting a sprint." `start`
(`setMachine` in `cmd/nova-sprint/run.go`) runs the check and refuses on a violation; the gate
carries DO NOT REMOVE WITHOUT GLENN'S EXPLICIT PERMISSION and no flag skips it. `run` does not
start the machine. The card checks (`check cards`, `startCardCheckGate`) only warn, and the needs
gate (`startNeedsGate`) is off on a mem twin; the sprint check (`st.Check`) runs on every store. Pinned by `TestStartRefusesWhileCheckFindsAViolation`, which plants a
violation.

## R-PLAYBOOK (H12)

The owner, 2026-10-18: "The coordinator must always refresh the playbook so they know how to
operate the sprint machine." `nova-sprint help playbook` prints the coordinator's playbook
(`docs/COORDINATOR-PLAYBOOK.md`, compiled in), held to the verb table by
`TestHelpPlaybookPrintsTheCoordinatorPlaybook` and `TestThePlaybookNamesOnlyRealVerbsAndUsageLines`. The push that
arms a coordinator's hook is nova-hook's (nova-tools) and does not name the playbook yet: the
divergence names sprint-spec-fix-armed-push-names-playbook and its owed test in nova-tools.
