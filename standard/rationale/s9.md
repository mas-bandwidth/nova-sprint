# s9. The bus, hooks and pushes (rationale)

Status: pending. The section's reasons are written with it.

## R-HOOKED (H10)

The owner, 2026-10-18: "The coordinator and all friends must hook in during the sprint to get
push notifications." The hook is nova-hook's (nova-tools); its challenge is the never-remove
10-minute hook challenge of the sprint's gates. A friend's status is the hook record's
(`FriendHookStatus`): a missing, unproven or down hook is down, and a down row is dealt nothing.
The coordinator's verbs are refused with "You must hook in first" until its hook is proven
(`CoordinatorGate`, `hookFirst`; DO NOT REMOVE WITHOUT GLENN'S EXPLICIT PERMISSION). The tests that hold these halves today, and pin the row's enforced part, are
`TestFriendHookStatus` (a missing, unproven or down hook is down), `TestAnUnhookedFriendIsNotDealt`
and `TestATickDealsNothingToAnUnhookedFriend` (a down friend is dealt nothing), and
`TestEveryVerbRefusesTheCoordinatorUntilHooked` and `TestTheCoordinatorGateReadsTheNovaHookRecord`
(the coordinator's verbs refuse until its hook is proven). No test asserts that the coordinator is
told when a friend's hook goes down, so that part is a divergence naming sprint-spec-fix-hooked,
which is not yet a card. The exempt verbs are `hookExempt` in `cmd/nova-sprint/coordinator_hook.go`
(run, tick, server switch, seat install, selftest land; `hookExemptCall` gates `tick
--answer-rules`), and help, -h and version run before the gate (`main.go`). The row names them
because a reader of "every verb" would otherwise expect `run` to refuse; that a verb outside the
list refuses is what `TestEveryVerbRefusesTheCoordinatorUntilHooked` asserts over the whole verb
registry, `tick --answer-rules` included.

## R-PUSH-ONLY and the push classes (H11)

The owner, 2026-10-18: "All communications must be push notifications, not polling. Friends and
coordinators should never need to remember to poll anything." The machine pushes on the bus
(nova-tools `pkg/bus`) through `a.bus` in `cmd/nova-sprint`. Read on 2026-10-18:

- deal: a friend's dealt cards wake her (`wakeFriend`, `wakeFriendPass` in friend sync); a member
  is pushed nothing and its pass is a timer poll (docs/SPEC-SPRINT.md, "Push, not poll"):
  divergence sprint-spec-fix-push-on-deal.
- read: a friend's read card rides the same wake with no test; a read levelled to a row pushes
  nothing: divergence sprint-spec-fix-push-on-deal.
- judgment: the coordinator's inbox push (`coordFresh`), first once when raised, then a warning
  while it waits (`TestAWaitingJudgmentIsWarnedOnTheBus`: at 10 s, then every 30 minutes): pinned.
- stuck, bound, stalled: they ride the judgment push as ordinary judgments, with no class of their
  own and no push to the row: divergence sprint-spec-fix-push-on-judgment. The owed names for deal,
  read and bound follow that card's and sprint-spec-fix-push-on-deal's DONE-WHEN; stuck and stalled
  keep names of the row's own scope (an operation stuck, naming where; a card, stream or landing
  making no progress), because the card's names (`TestAStuckCardIsPushedWhenItSticks`,
  `TestAStalledJudgmentIsPushedAgain`) are narrower than the rows: a test of those names alone does
  not pin them.
- finish-due: no push exists and no card is named: divergence sprint-spec-fix-push-finish-due, not
  yet a card.
- friend-state: a member's state judgment reaches the coordinator; a friend's up, down or held is
  not pushed to her nor tested for the coordinator: divergence sprint-spec-fix-push-friend-state,
  not yet a card.
- landing: the merge step writes the landed note to the coordinator (`landingPush`), and
  `TestEveryLandingIsPushedToTheCoordinator` calls `landingPush` directly: no test asserts that
  every landing emits one, after the base holds it, and that it reaches the bus. Divergence
  sprint-spec-fix-push-landing, not yet a card.
- ci: the CI receipt to the seat and the card's owner (`ciTell`): pinned for a card's head
  (`TestCIReceiptTellsTheOwnerAndSeat`, a receipt naming a finished head). No test sends a result
  on a base: that part is a divergence naming sprint-spec-fix-push-ci-on-base, not yet a card.
