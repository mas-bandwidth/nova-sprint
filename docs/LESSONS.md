# Lessons from v1 (the tracer bullet)

What nova-sprint v1 taught, so v2 does not learn it again. One line per lesson: the rule; `guards:` the v1 failure it prevents, with its date or source; `v2:` PLACED in the v2 design file that already says it, or MISSING. Sources: v1 `docs/` and the root ledgers at `origin/main` of nova-sprint, Rowan's memory notes, and the kin's v2 reviews of 2026-11-06 (section 3). Priority (LAND > FIX > READ > WORK), merging wide, and tiers and routes are in [LESSONS-PRIORITY-AND-MERGE.md](LESSONS-PRIORITY-AND-MERGE.md) (cited below as LPM with its W, D, M or T number). They are referenced here, not repeated. The designs cite a lesson as LESSONS <section>.<n>, where n counts the lines of its section from 1: sections 1 to 6 are the layers, C is the card contract, and W is "How we work".

## Layer 1: the store

- Each fact has one writer, and every reader reads that record and never computes it again. guards: friend liveness kept in 8+ records computed 6 ways, disagreeing live (hook said 7 up, sprint said 0), and the dashboard showing every friend down while `where` showed them up (2026-10-14, 10-15). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (one `sprint` role is the only writer; presence is the bus's fact).
- One operation is one transaction, with its event row in the same commit; no partial state, no repair pass. guards: the v1 multi-table fence, pending operations and "repair skipped changes" judgments (SPEC-SPRINT section 10); the lander pushed a commit, then failed to record it (2026-10-19). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (guarantee 1).
- Ops are idempotent by op id, and a transport failure is reported as unknown, never as unchanged. guards: the card-layer ruling of 2026-09-29 (part 4); verbs told they were cut when another writer had finished their op (SPEC-SPRINT section 10). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (guarantees 2 and 3, `Replay`).
- A refused move is logged as an event. guards: v1 did not record refusals; fix "A refused verb is written on the server's own log, with its refusal". v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md.
- Replaying the log rebuilds the state. guards: SPEC-SPRINT section 9 rule 13, which only `check` held and the tick never read. v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (guarantee 5, rebuild functional test).
- No heartbeats go in the store or the log; presence comes from the bus at tick time. guards: v1's log full of beats; ~1,100 server batches a minute, 900 of them friend daemons polling `friend cards` every second (2026-10-17). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (no heartbeat column).
- Writers are serialized, and a condition is checked against the rows as they are at commit. guards: the stale-check lost update (Store.tla witness); "a second tick must not move a card the first deal placed" (old roadmap). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (one lock, guarantee 7).
- Every plan is fenced on the gen of each row it read. guards: an old member kept working a moved card's old generation (fix "the old member stops the old generation within one beat"). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (`CardGen`, `RowGen`).
- A clear is one epoch increment. History stays readable, rows and policy carry forward, work does not. guards: fix "A clear is tabula rasa for the work, never for the readers"; `init` after a clear left the readers table empty and every read at "cannot ask" (2026-10-20). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md.
- An op planned in an old epoch is refused. guards: AttemptsEpochs "late writer" witness; Johnny: "do not let a rule change, a stop, or a clear rewrite a card already in my hand" (review 2026-11-06). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (op epoch check).
- Live holders never exceed width, enforced in the store on every path that assigns work. guards: hetzner1 at 20/16 (2026-10-18). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (the holder write is the check).
- Width has one source (nova-config) and reaches the store with no hand sync. guards: width came from `fleet up --width`, seat defaults and lane counts; a nova-config change did nothing until `fleet sync` was run by hand (2026-10-18, 10-20). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (rows: width written only by the run loop from nova-config) and LAYER-2-CARD-MACHINE.md (`Width`).
- The store refuses a schema version it does not know, naming both numbers. guards: fix "The server and its shadow refuse a store missing the locked columns" (v1 `TABLES.lock`). v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (`sprint.meta`).
- Time is passed in by the caller; there is one clock comparison, and deadlines count running time (STOPPED spans excluded). guards: SPEC-SPRINT section 19, one `DueNow` for every deadline, timer, overdue mark and wait ("there is no second clock comparison in the tree"), with STOPPED time not counted. v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md for time passed in; running-time deadlines MISSING.
- The store and the snapshot are measured at 4x live size (thousands of cards, many judgments). guards: tick cost grew as judgments x cards (2026-10-18); `keepWhere` regex-parsed every card on every tick, 12% of CPU (2026-10-17); fix "The tick stays under one second at load". v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (the load test at 4x, bound held unmeasured until measured).
- A backup and restore verb exists, and a reset is backed up first. guards: v1 `sprint-backup-verb`, `store-snapshot-verb`; old roadmap "a reset to zero is a backed-up verb". v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (backup and restore; the verb is layer 6's).
- Cost is recorded per card (reads, fixes and decide calls included), sums to the stream and the sprint, and an unpriced run is shown as unpriced, never $0. guards: the owner's cost column (2026-10-01, 10-11, "capture all cost per-card, on the card itself"); fix "A zero harness cost on an unpriced route is counted unpriced". v2: PLACED in LAYER-1-STORE.md (`cost`, `priced` on the consumer; totals are sums) and LAYER-2-CARD-MACHINE.md (`Report`).

1. A soft `after` must never hold a landing when its target is not queued. LAYER-2 as written skips the card, and in v1 that rule froze four streams' landings for two days.
2. Nothing stalls: a wait-for liveness model in which every waiting card has an actor, a deadline or a judgment. Each deadlock class cost hours, and v1 fixed them one at a time without converging.
3. Width caps work in progress, not what is queued. The v2 holder write counts `ready` consumers as live, which brings back the 10-18 starvation (heavy cards undealt while rows sat at width).
4. Environment, harness and bench faults spend no attempt and never count toward `Block`. Otherwise the new blocked state fires on infrastructure (48 environment exits per card on 11-05) and parks good cards.
5. A fix carries the previous head and the finding to the next attempt. Without it, every rework restarts from base and v1's waste returns, along with Emma's discarded branches.
6. A brief or rule edit never bounces or rewrites a card in hand. In v1, 156 bounces and lost reads came from edits, and v2 has no edit move designed yet.
7. A red base gets one judgment, lands its own fix first and resumes every stream on green. LANDING only prevents red; a base that is already red would stop every stream with no path back.
8. Ledger files are merged entry by entry by the lander. Without that, the new path-overlap rule serializes every card that touches fixes.sexp, the TLA tables or BASE-REDS.
9. Idle and landing-stall alarms are pushed. In v1, 561 held cards and 30 minutes with no landing went unseen until a person looked, and v2 has no time-bounded push for either.
10. No network I/O or whole-history read happens while the single writer or the run loop is held. One slow `git clone` under the control line stalled the whole fleet (10-25), and v2's one-lock store makes the same mistake easy.
