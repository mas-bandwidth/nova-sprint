# Learn from earlier reviews

These are recorded read and use reviews from earlier Nova Tools sprint and
work-tree versions. They moved with the nova-sprint repository. Keep the
reviewers' words and results intact: a friendly writing pass should not
rewrite what someone observed.

Use them to find confusing handoffs, compare earlier expectations with the
current implementation, or understand why a rule was added. Their version
and snapshot labels describe the reviewed artifact; they are not a current
nova-sprint release certification.

- [1.1.0 sprint read](1.1.0/sprint-read.md) and [sprint use](1.1.0/sprint-use.md).
- [1.1.0 work-tree read](1.1.0/work-read.md) and [work-tree use](1.1.0/work-use.md).
- Additional named reviewers are under `1.1.0/emma`, `1.1.0/johnny`, and
  `1.1.0/zhi`; pinned reports are under `snapshots`.
