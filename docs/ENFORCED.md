# Enforcement Ledger — Layers 1, 2 and 5

Nothing ceiling: 20

This ledger records every guarantee, rule, refusal, card-contract clause, and `PLACED` v1 lesson claimed by Layer 1 (`docs/LAYER-1-STORE.md`), Layer 2 (`docs/LAYER-2-CARD-MACHINE.md`) and Layer 5 (`docs/LAYER-5-POLICY.md`), together with the concrete artifacts in the tree that hold it today:

- `invariant <Module>.<Name>` — TLA+ invariant in `tla/<Module>.tla` checked by `tla/*.cfg`
- `witness <Name>` — reversed TLA+ witness in `tla/witnesses/<Name>.tla` (or reach check in `tla/reach/<Name>.cfg`)
- `test <TestName>[/<scenario>]` — Go test in `store/*_test.go`, `card/*_test.go` or `policy/*_test.go`
- `check <Name>` — PostgreSQL `CHECK` constraint, `UNIQUE` constraint/index, `FOREIGN KEY`, or trigger in `store/schema.sql`
- `nothing` — claimed by Layer 1 or Layer 2 documentation, but not yet held by any model, test, or schema check
