# Run actual-code trace contract

RunTrace consumes a code-emitted RunTraceLog TLA module defining TraceLog as a
sequence of records. Records have 1-based i and action; process actions also
have p, push actions r, Report c, DeliverEdge j. Required(r) in RunTrace.tla
lists the necessary projected fields for each action. Other listed fields
may be logged and must match when present. Hidden model state is not evidence
about unobserved code. The fixed initial fixture uses p1/p2, recipient r1,
three jobs on distinct cards and budget two; configuration changes are explicit.

## Production state versus dependency fixture

The actual chooser/Engine/Pusher execute. Scheduler and external transports
may be injected deterministic test dependencies. Job IDs are assigned by first
actual scheduler admission, not generated by stepping Run.tla. Model gens are
tracked card generations relative to the fixture's observed initial baseline.
Done/counts come from accepted unique tracked operations and store state.

Run.events contains accepted tracked moves/reports. Store also logs refused
operations, cursor writes and bootstrap rows. Project cursor and fold positions
to the rank of tracked relevant events at or before the observed physical seq.
Filtering irrelevant rows is explicit; a refused tracked operation still emits
Refuse with its actual receipt and changed pending/refused queues. State-changing
or recipient-relevant events cannot silently disappear through the filter.
The first fixture requires every tracked event to have a notice for r1.

procBatch/procPutback/procApplied project actual pending operation IDs,
recorded refusals and accepted count. procCopy projects the conditions on the
actual returned plan, not arbitrary current card generations. procFold projects
the folded snapshot sequence. procPhase follows observed synchronous lifecycle
boundaries. Model procCarry/seen are work-queue abstractions supplied by the
scheduler fixture; production Plan.Carry/inbox are edge facts, not these job IDs.
Binding fixture queue order does not prove production Layer3 carry semantics.

## Sampling boundaries

Acquire callbacks expose PG lock acquisition then hook acquisition/cleanup.
Steps.Tick entry observes the completed fold; first Store.Apply entry after
plan return can observe the real installed pending plan (Select). Subsequent
fold/operation callbacks observe actual receipt handling and pending advancement
(Apply or Refuse). TickEdges.Committed entry observes the completed plan,
carried inbox and cleared pending pointer (EndTick). If these callbacks cannot
expose a boundary, add a safe observation hook or report the missing boundary;
never fabricate intermediate states just to fit model action names.

Pusher observations must distinguish prepared committed prefix, actual confirmed
recipient/message-ID response, and persisted cursor. A transport call returning
success is insufficient until the real code's validation path has accepted it.
Source-owned lifecycle observations and fake external-state observations must
remain distinguishable in evidence. Explicit unchanged Stutter is permitted
for irrelevant reads/replay-only rows, not state-changing tracked operations.

## Scope and refusal

The checked three seeded schedules bind tick/refusal/push, observed lock loss,
and restart with a fresh chooser under fake dependencies. Clear is separately
bound by RunClearTrace, including ten enabled-action selection walks. Unknown
Apply/replay outcomes and overflow summaries remain explicit gaps unless
exercised and mapped. An in-flight unknown
commit cannot be relabeled as a known receipt or hidden merely to obtain a pass.

TraceInit uses Run.Init and checks the observed first projection as an invariant,
avoiding a vacuous zero-initial-state pass. TraceMatched requires the final log
record to be reached through the existing model actions and logged states. The
first refusal is the record after the last matched index l. A finite matched
trace is evidence about that execution only, not general implementation safety
or liveness. Preserve failures and diagnose model/source/refinement mismatches;
do not broaden Next or weaken invariants merely to accept a trace.
